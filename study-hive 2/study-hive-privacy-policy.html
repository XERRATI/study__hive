What data the app stores

Study Hive keeps your study data (subjects, minutes, sessions, notes, flashcards and settings) only in your own browser (localStorage on your device). Nothing is uploaded to a server by the app itself.

What data leaves your device

Only what you choose to send: feedback messages you submit, and anything you paste into AI-powered tools that call an external AI service. Background music and fonts are loaded from the internet (Google Fonts and your own audio file).
